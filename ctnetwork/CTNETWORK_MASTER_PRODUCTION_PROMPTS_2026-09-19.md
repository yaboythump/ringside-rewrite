# CTNETWORK MASTER PRODUCTION PROMPTS — 2026-09-19

## NETWORK-WIDE PRODUCTION LAW

When the owner says **START PRODUCTION**, begin the approved production immediately. Do not change the show formula while producing.

For all standard CTNETWORK shows:
1. Create the thumbnail and/or picture storyboard in ChatGPT according to the show's gate.
2. Do not proceed past required visual approval until the owner approves the visuals.
3. The CTNETWORK server is used **ONLY for narration**.
4. Do **NOT** use the server for image generation, video assembly, editing, motion, music, SFX, or final rendering unless the owner explicitly asks.
5. Assemble the finished episode outside the server from the approved ChatGPT visuals plus the approved narration.
6. Add show-specific music, SFX, selective motion, editing, audio mastering, and QC during direct assembly.
7. Return a playable finished master for owner approval.
8. Cut Shorts only from that exact approved master.
9. Nothing publishes without owner approval.
10. No burned captions unless the owner explicitly requests them.
11. Preserve native narrator speed. Do not artificially speed up or slow down voices.
12. Never silently substitute a narrator. A narrator failure blocks only that show's narration lane.
13. Thumbnail failure is a production error. Thumbnail-first rules are mandatory where specified.

## RINGSIDE RECAP
Use F06. The gate is mandatory and always in this order: **1) thumbnail, 2) picture storyboard approval.** Do not move to scripting or narration before those two visual steps are approved.

Every full Ringside Recap must run **4–5 minutes** and use **at least 16 distinct visuals**, with **18–22 preferred**. The edit must breathe like a real wrestling recap show: hold major images roughly **10–18 seconds**, use selective motion rather than constant cuts, and do not rush through match results. Cover the full episode story arc, not just the main event. Rapid-fire two-minute highlight pacing is forbidden.

The default music bed is permanently locked to **Ringside Recap Arena Rock Bed v1** at `assets/ringside-recap/Ringside_Recap_Arena_Rock_Bed.mp3`. Use this same bed in every Ringside Recap unless the owner explicitly changes it. The sound is aggressive modern arena rock / premium sports broadcast: real electric guitars, live-sounding punchy drums, bass, and restrained cinematic hits. **No video-game music, synthwave, chiptune, arcade tones, or EDM lead synths.** Duck the bed under F06 narration, and add ring-bell and crowd SFX where appropriate. F06 stays at native speed and must sound natural, never robotic. Shorts are cut only from the approved master.
