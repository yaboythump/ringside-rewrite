## Lyrics + dance cues
**[Intro — Jojo spoken]** Hey friends! It's Tiny Turn Up! Thanksgiving dance time — let's TURN UP!

**[Verse 1]**
Flap your wings like a turkey, flap flap flap *(elbows out, flap arms)*
Shake your tail feathers, give 'em a clap *(wiggle hips, clap twice)*
Strut to the left now, strut strut strut *(big steps left, chin out)*
Strut to the right — now wiggle that butt! *(steps right, wiggle)*

**[Chorus]**
Gobble gobble WOBBLE! *(wobble whole body like jelly)*
Gobble gobble WOBBLE! *(wobble)*
Wobble to the floor and wobble back up *(squat down slowly, pop up)*
Gobble gobble WOBBLE! *(wobble + jump)*

**[Verse 2 — Kiki: "Watch THIS!"]**
Peck peck peck like you're looking for corn *(bob head forward)*
Stomp your turkey feet, loud as a horn *(stomp stomp)*
Spin around once — whoa, don't fall down! *(spin)*
*(Waddles spins too much and tumbles — "Quack!")*

**[Chorus]** ×1

**[Bridge — FREEZE]**
When the music stops, turkeys FREEZE! *(music stops 3 sec — freeze)*
...and gobble! *(music returns)* ×3 freezes

**[Chorus]** ×2, faster the second time

**[Outro]** High five! Happy Thanksgiving! See you next dance!
