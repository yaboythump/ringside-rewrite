## Lyrics + dance cues
**[Intro — Jojo in a scarf]** Hey friends! It's snowing at Tiny Turn Up! Let's build a dancing snowman! Let's TURN UP!

**[Verse 1 — build the snowman]**
Roll a big snowball, roll roll roll *(roll arms)*
Stack it up high, that's the snowman's goal *(stack fists upward)*
Carrot nose — boop! *(touch nose)*
Two button eyes — blink blink! *(point at eyes, blink)*

**[Chorus]**
Do the snowman shake! *(shake all over)*
Shiver shiver shake! *(shiver, teeth chatter "brrr")*
Shake your arms like sticks *(stiff arms, shake)*
Shake your snowy feet *(shake each foot)*
Do the snowman shake!

**[Bridge — melting]**
Uh oh, the sun came out... we're MELTING... *(slowly melt to floor)*
Now it's snowing again — POP back up! *(jump up)*
*(Waddles melts flat like a puddle — "Quack?")*

**[Speed round]** Snowman shake... slow *(slow motion)*... medium... SUPER FAST! *(fast shake)*

**[Final chorus]**

**[Outro]** Brrr! High five! See you next dance!
