## Lyrics + dance cues
**[Intro — Jojo in a Santa hat]** Hey friends! It's Christmas at Tiny Turn Up! Jingle when the music plays — FREEZE when it stops! Let's TURN UP!

**[Verse 1]**
Jingle your hands like bells, ring-a-ding *(shake hands like bells)*
Gallop like a reindeer, let's go, let's swing *(gallop in place)*
Stretch up tall like a Christmas tree *(arms up, point to sky)*
Now... FREEZE! *(stop 3 sec)*

**[Chorus]**
Jingle jingle FREEZE! *(freeze)*
Jingle jingle GO! *(dance)*
Shake it like a snow globe, wiggle in the snow! *(shake whole body)*
Jingle jingle FREEZE! *(freeze)* — Merry Christmas, ho ho ho!

**[Verse 2 — Kiki: "Watch THIS!"]**
Wrap yourself like a present, spin around *(hug self, spin)*
Tiptoe like an elf, don't make a sound *(tiptoe, finger on lips)*
Slide like a sled — wheee! *(slide side to side)*
*(Waddles slides away off screen — "Quaaack!")*

**[Freeze round — "Freeze like a…"]**
...SNOWMAN! ...REINDEER! ...CANDY CANE! (bend sideways) ...SLEEPING SANTA!

**[Final chorus ×2]**

**[Outro]** High five! Merry Christmas, friends! See you next dance!
