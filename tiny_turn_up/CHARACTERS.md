# Cast & Style Guide

Paste the **style line** + the character descriptions into every image/thumbnail prompt so they stay consistent.

**Style line:** `Bright 3D animated kids cartoon style, glossy Pixar-like rendering, saturated purple/yellow/teal palette, sunny lighting, big expressive eyes, joyful energy, clean simple background, no text unless specified.`

## Jojo (lead dancer)
Boy, about 6, warm brown skin, big curly black afro, huge smile. Yellow overalls over a white T-shirt, teal high-top sneakers with a star on the sole.
Personality: loud, brave, always starts the dance. Catchphrase: **"Let's TURN UP!"**

## Kiki (best friend)
Girl, about 5, brown skin, two puffy pigtails with purple flower clips. Lavender overalls over a white T-shirt with a yellow flower, pink sneakers.
Personality: the move-maker who invents new steps. Catchphrase: **"Watch THIS!"**

## Waddles (the duck)
Small fluffy yellow duckling with a teal bow tie and orange feet. Always a beat behind and falls over on "freeze" (the running gag kids love).
Catchphrase: **"Quack-quack, freeze back!"**

## Home base
Colorful city playground court: purple floor, graffiti-style crown murals, basketball hoop, brick buildings, sunny sky. Holiday episodes redecorate it (leaves, snow, lights).

## Signature bits (use in every song)
- Opens with Jojo: "Hey friends! It's Tiny Turn Up! Let's TURN UP!"
- Waddles messes up once per song.
- Ends with: "High five! See you next dance!" + end screen to the next song.
