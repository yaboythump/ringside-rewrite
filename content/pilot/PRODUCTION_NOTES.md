# Pilot Production Notes

Episode: **What If Sting Led the WCW Invasion?**

Slug: `sting-leads-the-invasion`

Core promise: begin with documented wrestling history, say exactly when the timeline becomes fictional, then fantasy-book the WCW Invasion like a serious long-form sports documentary.

Documented starting point:

- WWE announced the WCW acquisition on March 23, 2001.
- WCW's final Nitro aired March 26, 2001.
- The actual Invasion became a WCW/ECW Alliance angle and ended at Survivor Series 2001.
- Sting did not debut in WWE until Survivor Series 2014.

Rewrite lane:

- Sting refuses to be a conquered trophy.
- WCW becomes a protected Resistance instead of a comedy undercard.
- ECW is a third side, not WCW's backup army.
- Survivor Series decides whether WCW earns its own weekly hour.
- Sting wins the war, Rock wins the WrestleMania recognition match, and the 2002 brand split becomes sharper.

Production guardrails:

- Do not write direct quotes for real wrestlers.
- Keep visuals illustrated, editorial, and unofficial.
- Avoid official promotion logos, belts with trademarks, readable arena signage, or deceptive real-broadcast styling.
- Keep the AI/synthetic-media disclosure in metadata and description.
