# Ringside Rewrite — Brand Playbook

Tagline: **Wrestling History. Booked Differently.**

## Channel position

Ringside Rewrite publishes unofficial wrestling what-if fantasy booking. The channel should feel like a premium sports documentary crossed with a booker's war room: researched, dramatic, opinionated, and easy for wrestling fans to argue with in the comments.

The formula:

1. Start with documented wrestling history.
2. State exactly where the timeline becomes fictional.
3. Rewrite the booking with consequences, payoffs, and clear stakes.
4. End with a viewer booking question.

## Voice

Confident Black male sports-documentary narrator. Conversational, dramatic, controlled, and never doing an impression of a wrestler, announcer, promoter, or commentator.

Avoid:

- Fake quotes from real people
- Rumors presented as facts
- Contract, injury, or private motive claims without sourcing
- Official logos or deceptive broadcast visuals
- Comedy-first burial of wrestlers or promotions

## Episode structure

- Hook: one impossible wrestling question
- Receipts: documented baseline and source-backed setup
- Cutoff: clear line where the rewrite starts
- Booking: multi-event arc with escalation
- Reversal: one major surprise that changes the board
- Consequence: how the timeline stays different afterward
- Question: ask viewers how they would book it

## Recurring buckets

- Invasion rewrites
- Missed dream matches
- Wrestler trajectory changes
- Championship reign fixes
- Promotion war alternate histories
- Faction breakups that should have gone differently
- Pay-per-view card rewrites

## Disclaimers

Every episode description should say the channel is unofficial, fan-made, AI-assisted, and not affiliated with any wrestling promotion.
