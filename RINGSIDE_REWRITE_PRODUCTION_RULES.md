# Ringside Rewrite — Locked Production Formula

## Permanent visual workflow
Ringside Rewrite uses the same collage-first production formula as Hip Hop What If.

1. Thumbnail/collage first.
2. Treat the approved collage as the visual source of truth and storyboard.
3. Extract the major visual beats directly from the collage.
4. Build LTX scene prompts from those exact visual beats.
5. Do not invent an unrelated parallel shot list unless Thump explicitly asks for extra scenes.
6. Render the LTX scene package.
7. Show the actual rendered scenes to Thump for approval before master assembly.
8. Only after scene approval, assemble the full episode using the locked Ringside Rewrite narrator, pacing, music, branding, and alternate-history storytelling formula.
9. Cut exactly 3 Shorts from the approved master unless Thump explicitly changes the count.
10. Do not upload or publish anything until Thump explicitly approves it.

## Core rule
Same Hip Hop What If visual workflow, different brand:

- HHWI: topic collage/storyboard -> LTX scenes -> scene approval -> full episode -> Shorts.
- Ringside Rewrite: wrestling collage/storyboard -> LTX scenes -> scene approval -> full episode -> 3 Shorts.

## Guardrail
The collage is not just the thumbnail. It is the production storyboard.

If an LTX render drifts away from the approved collage's subjects, relationships, era, visual beats, or implied alternate-history direction, discard/redesign that render rather than building the episode around it.

Do not replace collage-driven scenes with generic wrestling B-roll, unrelated wrestlers, random alternate-history detours, or visually impressive but off-story shots.

## Approval gate
The scene review must contain the actual rendered LTX scenes, not text descriptions or storyboard mockups. Thump can approve all scenes or call out specific scene numbers for regeneration. Nothing advances to narration/master assembly until that review is approved.
