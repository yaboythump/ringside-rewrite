# Ringside Rewrite Launch Slate

Pilot: **What If Sting Led the WCW Invasion?**

## First 10 episodes

| # | Episode | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What If Sting Led the WCW Invasion? | WCW gets a real leader, real stakes, and one weekly hour to fight for. |
| 2 | What If The Rock Never Left for Hollywood? | WWE's 2003-2005 main-event scene changes around a full-time Rock. |
| 3 | What If The Undertaker Lost at WrestleMania Earlier? | The streak breaks years sooner, forcing WWE to rebuild its myth-making. |
| 4 | What If CM Punk Won the Royal Rumble in 2014? | The Yes Movement collides with Punk's last stand instead of replacing it. |
| 5 | What If Goldberg Debuted in WWE the Right Way? | The WCW monster arrives protected, focused, and dangerous from night one. |
| 6 | What If The Shield Never Split in 2014? | Roman, Seth, and Dean reshape WWE as a dominant faction for another year. |
| 7 | What If Bret Hart Never Left WWE in 1997? | The Attitude Era still happens, but the power structure changes completely. |
| 8 | What If Daniel Bryan Beat The Authority Sooner? | The WrestleMania 30 build catches fire before the company can resist it. |
| 9 | What If NXT Beat AEW in the Wednesday Night War? | WWE treats black-and-gold NXT like a true third brand instead of a feeder. |
| 10 | What If Cody Rhodes Won at WrestleMania 39? | The Bloodline story ends earlier, creating a different championship era. |

## Rotation rule

Do not repeat the same primary wrestler, faction, or promotion-centered hook inside three episodes unless the audience specifically asks for a series.

## Shorts strategy

Each long-form episode creates three Shorts:

- The impossible question
- The booking fix
- The comment-bait verdict
