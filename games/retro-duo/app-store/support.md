# AVYDRIX Play Support

AVYDRIX Play is the CTInteractive retro gaming console for iPhone.

Use **Add Games** to import compatible game files you are legally entitled to use. AVYDRIX Play does not provide commercial ROM downloads. Game compatibility varies by system and title.

Open **Settings** for console skins, display modes, audio, controller mappings and About & open-source licenses. Use the in-game **Command Center** to save, load, adjust controls or return to the library. Back up your game and save files before reinstalling the app.

For help, [open a support issue](https://github.com/yaboythump/ringside-rewrite/issues/new) with “AVYDRIX Play” in the title, your app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, affected system and reproduction steps. Do not upload copyrighted ROMs, private saves or personal information. GitHub issues are public.
