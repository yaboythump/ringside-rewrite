# AVYDRIX Play Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026. AVYDRIX Play is a CTInteractive application.

## Your games stay on your device
Imported games, save files, automatic resume checkpoints, selected covers, favorites, recently played history, controller mappings, console preferences and player profile are stored in the app’s local container. CTInteractive does not operate an account service or receive these files or profile information through the app. Device backups and file sharing are controlled by you and your operating system.

## Cover artwork requests
The app can look up matching game covers using game names. These HTTPS requests go to raw.githubusercontent.com and thumbnails.libretro.com. The providers receive the requested game name and normal connection information such as your IP address and may process server logs under their own policies. Game ROM contents, saves and player profiles are not sent. You can choose your own cover from your device; cached covers remain available offline.

## Tracking and permissions
The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. Files and images you select are used to import your games and covers. Controller access supports local gameplay. No microphone, contacts or location access is required.

## Your choices
You control imports and deletion through the app and iOS Files. Removing the app may remove its local games and saves; back up files you want to keep first. Contact CTInteractive through the support page for privacy questions. Do not attach private games, saves or personal information to public support reports.
