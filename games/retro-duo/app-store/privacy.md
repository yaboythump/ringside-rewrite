# AVYDRIX Play Privacy Policy

Updated November 23, 2026. AVYDRIX Play is a CTInteractive application.

## Your games stay on your device
Imported games, save files, automatic resume checkpoints, selected covers, favorites, recently played history, controller mappings, console preferences and player profile are stored in the app’s local container. CTInteractive does not operate an account service or receive these files or profile information through the app. Device backups and file sharing are controlled by you and your operating system.

## Cover artwork requests
The app can look up matching game covers using game names. These HTTPS requests go to raw.githubusercontent.com and thumbnails.libretro.com. The providers receive the requested game name and normal connection information such as your IP address and may process server logs under their own policies. Game ROM contents, saves and player profiles are not sent. You can choose your own cover from your device; cached covers remain available offline.

## Optional live streaming
When you choose Go live, the app sends the game picture and game audio directly to the streaming server you select (Twitch, YouTube, Kick or another RTMP/RTMPS service). If you enable the microphone and grant iOS permission, your voice is included. The destination receives your stream key and ordinary connection information, including your IP address, and can associate the broadcast with your streaming account. Stream keys are stored in the device's Keychain; server addresses and quality preferences are stored locally. CTInteractive does not operate a relay, streaming account service or broadcast archive.

Your chosen service controls broadcast visibility, audience access, recordings, retention and deletion under its own settings and policies. A stream may be public or retained by that service. Use End stream to stop transmission; leaving the app also ends the stream. Ending a stream does not delete recordings already retained by the destination. Manage or delete those in that service, and revoke or rotate a stream key there if needed. Only broadcast content you have permission to share. Gameplay does not require streaming or microphone permission.

## Tracking and permissions
The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. Files and images you select are used to import your games and covers. Controller access supports local gameplay. Microphone access is optional and used only for voice in a live broadcast. Contacts and location access are not required.

## Your choices
You control imports and deletion through the app and iOS Files. Removing the app may remove its local games and saves; back up files you want to keep first. Contact CTInteractive through the support page for privacy questions. Do not attach private games, saves or personal information to public support reports.
