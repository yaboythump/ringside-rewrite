Ringside Combo

Wrestling meets match-3. Pick your wrestler.

  • Swap neighbouring tiles to match 3 or more. Every match is a move.
  • Match 4 → Clothesline tile (clears a line). Match 5 → Finisher 🌀.
  • Swap the Finisher with any tile to hit it. If it lands with the opponent under 30% health, you go for the pin.
  • During a pin, clear enough tiles before the ref counts 3.
  • ⏱ Don't stall! If the move clock runs out, your opponent gets a free hit.
  • 🦶 Kicks break the opponent's guard 🛡. Match next to 🪑 chairs or 🥊 knucks to clear them.
