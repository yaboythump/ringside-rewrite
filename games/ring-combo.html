Ringside Combo

Wrestling meets match-3. Pick your wrestler.

  • It's turn-based: you make one move, then your opponent makes one on the same board. Their matches hit you.
  • Swap neighbouring tiles to match 3 or more.
  • Match 4 → Clothesline tile (clears a line). Match 5 → Finisher 🌀.
  • Swap your gold-ringed Finisher with any tile to hit it. Red-ringed ones belong to your opponent. If yours lands with them under 30% health, you go for the pin.
  • During a pin, clear enough tiles before the ref counts 3.
  • Hit 0 health and they cover you: clear enough tiles in one move to kick out.
  • ⏱ Don't stall! If your turn clock runs out, you lose your turn.
  • 🦶 Kicks break the opponent's guard 🛡. Match next to 🪑 chairs or 🥊 knucks to clear them.
